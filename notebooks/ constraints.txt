# Orcaopta Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, safe, and collaborative environment for everyone.  
Orcaopta is an open‑source community built on respect, curiosity, and a shared passion for reliability, automation, and intelligent cloud systems.

We pledge to make participation in our project and community a harassment‑free experience for all, regardless of:

- Age  
- Body size  
- Disability  
- Ethnicity  
- Gender identity and expression  
- Level of experience  
- Nationality  
- Personal appearance  
- Race  
- Religion  
- Sexual identity and orientation  
- Socioeconomic status  

We want Orcaopta to be a place where people feel comfortable contributing, learning, and building together.

---

## Our Standards

### **Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:**

- Being respectful and inclusive  
- Actively listening to others  
- Offering constructive feedback  
- Helping newcomers  
- Showing empathy and understanding  
- Focusing on what’s best for the community  
- Celebrating contributions and progress  

### **Examples of unacceptable behavior:**

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks  
- Trolling, insults, or derogatory comments  
- Publishing private information without permission  
- Threatening or violent language  
- Disruptive behavior in discussions or pull requests  
- Sexualized language or imagery  
- Any conduct that makes others feel unsafe or unwelcome  

---

## Our Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for:

- Clarifying standards of acceptable behavior  
- Reviewing and moderating contributions  
- Taking appropriate action in response to unacceptable behavior  
- Removing, editing, or rejecting comments, commits, issues, or PRs that violate this Code of Conduct  
- Temporarily or permanently banning contributors who repeatedly violate the rules  

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- The Orcaopta GitHub repository  
- Discussions  
- Issues and pull requests  
- Community spaces (Discord, Slack, forums, etc.)  
- Any public interaction related to the project  

---

## Enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately:

**Email:** `security@orcaopta.dev`  
(If you don’t have this email yet, use your personal email temporarily.)

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

Maintainers will:

- Respect confidentiality  
- Respond within a reasonable timeframe  
- Take action appropriate to the situation  

---

## Consequences

Violations may result in:

- Warning  
- Temporary ban  
- Permanent ban  
- Removal of contributions  
- Escalation to platform administrators (GitHub, etc.)

The severity depends on the nature of the violation.
